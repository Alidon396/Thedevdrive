## Conversion Success Indicators

### Signs a Lead is Warm 🔥
✅ Opens email (track via demo link clicks)  
✅ Requests call or asks questions  
✅ Asks about timeline or modification  
✅ Mentions budget or availability  
✅ Shares demo with team/colleagues  

### Objection Patterns to Watch 🚨
- "Too expensive" → Offer payment plan
- "I'll think about it" → Follow up day 3
- "Can you add more features?" → Scope creep warning
- "We already have someone" → Highlight speed & quality
- "No timeline" → Create urgency with expires-in announcement
